# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents of infants, toddlers and preschoolers in San Francisco, many of them Filipino-American, choosing a small home childcare. They compare providers, want to see the space and the caregivers, and want to book a tour.

## Product Purpose
Marketing and enrollment site for Mae's Childcare, a licensed home childcare (License #384005044) at 949 Grove Street, San Francisco. Success is a parent booking a visit or sending an enrollment inquiry.

## Positioning
Capacity of 8 children, bilingual English and Filipino (Tagalog), art-based Creative Curriculum, CPR and First Aid certified staff, daily nutritious meals, ages 4 months to 5 years. Mon-Fri 8:30 AM-5:30 PM.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js 15 App Router, Tailwind 4, Resend contact API. Routes: home, about, programs, staff, gallery, contact, enrollment. Copy, license number, contact details and form behavior are factual and stay as they are.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the existing logo (public/images/name.png, logo-only.svg) and the existing palette (orange #FF6B35, teal #2AB7CA, yellow #FED766, navy #004E89, cream #FFF9F0, sky #AEDFF7). Premium comes from type, layout, motion and craft, not from new brand colors.

## Evidence on Hand
Real photos in public/images (IMG_08xx-09xx.jpeg, teacher1-3.png). No testimonials or reviews exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Trust first: license, safety and the small group are shown plainly.
- Real photos of the real space beat illustration.
- One clear action: book a tour.
- Warm and calm, not loud.
